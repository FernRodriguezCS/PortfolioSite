Current build

Events are the starting point.

The mobile app now has a home, event, search, member, and preferences area. Here’s what the source currently supports.

  • Discover

    Browse events

    Filter upcoming events by date range, open details, and review event information.

  • Search

    Find a useful fit

    Search titles, descriptions, business names, or hashtags; filter by date, cost, or event type, and sort by upcoming date or title.

  • My events

    Review RSVP history

    See upcoming and past RSVP views, currently populated with sample records.

  • Member preview

    Explore member tools

    Preview a sample member dashboard and visitor view. Membership sign-in is not connected.
